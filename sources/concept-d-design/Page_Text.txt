In-person attendance is by invitation. If you have received an invitation, register using the email address it was sent to.

The draft event plan includes a virtual option open to everyone, with main-stage sessions livestreamed. Viewing details will be shared through the final registration page.

How do I request an accommodation?

Contact events@vectorinstitute.ai to discuss your needs. Venue accessibility details will be confirmed with the final event information.
