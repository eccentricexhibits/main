Home

The page that the registration email and social posts link to. Its job is to get people to register, in person or virtually.

  1. 1 · Nav

    Bilingual Vector logo; Home, Agenda, Speakers, FAQ; EN / FR switch; Register button. On mobile the links fold into a menu icon, and the switch and Register stay visible.

  2. 2 · Hero

    Event name, tagline, dates, venue and address. Two buttons: Register in person and Join virtually, with the invitation-only note under them. The dashed box is reserved for the 10th-anniversary mark the agency will supply.

  3. 3 · Intro

    Opening copy from the reg-site doc, updated for 2027. Left-aligned with a slight indent, as on vectorinstitute.ai.

  4. 4 · Stats

    Remarkable 2026 by the numbers: 1,500+ attendees, 39 countries, 20 speakers, 50+ research posters.

  5. 5 · Two days

    Day 1 is a full day of keynotes and research talks; Day 2 is a half day of workshops, lightning talks and posters. Links to the Agenda page.

  6. 6 · Speakers

    Six featured speakers with photo, name, title and organization. Links to the Speakers page. 2026 speakers as stand-ins.

  7. 7 · Recap video

    The 2026 recap. Plays only when clicked.

  8. 8 · Photo carousel

    Nine 2026 event photos. Moves only with the arrows or a swipe, never on its own.

  9. 9 · Funders

    Government of Canada, Government of Ontario and CIFAR logos at equal height.

  10. 10 · Footer

    Ontario funding credit, event contact, #Remarkable2027, social links, Vector site links, privacy and recording notice. Same on every page.

Numbers on the wireframe match the notes.

Agenda

The full program for both days.

  1. 1 · Nav

    Same as Home, with Agenda marked as the current page.

  2. 2 · Page header

    Event name, dates and venue, the page title and a one-paragraph intro. Every inner page opens this way.

  3. 3 · Day tabs and sessions

    Day 1 / Day 2 tabs jump to each day. Each day opens with its description from the copy doc, then the sessions: time, type, title, short description, speakers and room, with a Livestreamed tag on main-stage sessions. Breaks and meals are quieter rows.

    Sample program: session titles, times, rooms and speakers are placeholders.

  4. 4 · Footer

    Same as Home.

FAQ

Answers to the questions the events inbox gets most, so attendees can help themselves.

  1. 1 · Nav

    Same as Home, with FAQ marked as the current page.

  2. 2 · Page header

    Title, plus a line pointing to events@vectorinstitute.ai for anything not covered.

  3. 3 · FAQ groups

    Ten questions in four groups: Registration, Program, Venue and travel, Privacy. Each question opens and closes; they're shown open here so the answers can be reviewed.

    To confirm: transit directions, the accessibility answer and the registration-email steps.

    Same as Home.
