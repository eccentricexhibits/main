What changed

  • Body rebuilt to the original 2012 shoot: narrow sloped shoulders, a long round belly hanging to the crotch, pink panties showing beneath it.

  • Sleeves are separate tubes set into the shoulder, as on the costume, so lifting the elbows no longer webs the torso into "wings".
