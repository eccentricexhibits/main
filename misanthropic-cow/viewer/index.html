What changed

  • Round 25 (static preview): the costume's sides come in a little under the udders and the belly swells out below, as in the original shoot (4 of 15), instead of one straight wall. The briefs pass under the crotch between the legs again: a gusset runs down the middle from front to back, and the seat's cleft is a soft crease that the fabric bridges. With the arms raised there are no more holes under the arms: the body stays solid where the arm is trimmed back to the rounded shoulder.
  • Round 24 · Udders pink underneath: the udders are pink all the way under: each stuffed cup's underside is cloth right down to the seam where it meets the belly, with no more fur under them. The fur still covers the tops as in round 23. The cups' outer sides now round back onto the body instead of rising as a cliff, and the bulge fades where the chest turns to face sideways, so no pink edge stands out beside the body.
  • Round 23 · Fur over the udders: the white fur now comes right up to each stuffed cup, as on the empty suit: pink shows only on the cup itself, a teardrop, and the fur covers the swelling round its top and outer edge, meeting the cloth flush at the seam. There is no more pink spreading over the upper chest towards the shoulders. The right-hand udder's outer teat (as you look at her) is lowered to the same height as the left-hand one's.
  • Round 22 · Teats: the teats are placed as they were sewn on in the original shoot (close-ups in shots 4 and 5 of 15). They sit on the cloth itself rather than at the rim of each cup. The lower teat is low and a little inside of centre, hanging down and slightly forward; the outer one is on the cup's lower outer face, pointing out and forward (the cow's left one tilts up). They are shorter (about 6 cm), droop a little along their length, and differ slightly between the two sides, as hand-sewn ones do.
  • Round 21 · Udders: the udders are sized and stuffed as in the original photo shoot (shots 4 to 7 of 15), where the approved views drew them too small and too flat. Each is now about 22 cm wide and 24 cm tall, the pair filling the chest and meeting in the middle, and they are stuffed into full, round globes that stand out about 15 cm from the chest, heaviest low down. They are still sewn into the fur, with their teats re-seated.
  • Round 20 · Arms raised, spots and lighting: Arms raised (next to Compare) shows the same model with its arms lifted, so you can see the black fur under them. As on the real suit, the stuffed body keeps its shape: its shoulders are rounded, and the unstuffed sleeves turn out of them like a ball in a socket, with no folds. The white spots on the leggings are now each taken whole from the approved view that sees them most squarely, with the drawing's own brushed outline, and laid round the leg (spots seen side-on are no longer smeared or squashed). Lighting has five new rigs (Daylight, Golden hour, Moonlight, Stage, Backlit) and an Adjust row: brightness, turning the lights round the figure, raising or lowering them, cast shadows, and a choice of backdrop.
  • Round 19 · The new body: a new body. Its shape comes from your Tripo model, cleaned of Tripo's lumpy surface and fitted height by height to the outline of your four approved generated views. The legs keep the original photos' length. The revisions answer your notes. The head sits up on a visible throat, looking ahead, the hood falls straight from the crown to the shoulders at the back, and the shoulders slope away from the neck. Below the cups the midsection is now a stuffed sack, built like the empty suit in your photo: full and round down to a lower hem, with no waist and no bustle, over a round seat in the briefs; the bow sits just above the hem. Under the suit there is a person: the hips and thighs are a wearer's own, meeting in a soft groin crease. The udders are pink cloth panels sewn into holes in the fur and stuffed from behind, so they swell out of the suit with flush, stitched edges. The lower legs taper cleanly to a slim ankle, the heels stand on the slippers' footbeds, and the black shag and the leggings' white spots are clean tufted and painted shapes, in the approved views' layout. Your mask, horns, hood, gloves and slippers are unchanged. It has no rig yet, so it stands in the default pose; the animations come next. Compare it with round 17 under the picture.
  • Round 18 · Proportions from the original shoot: measured on the two full-length photos of the original session (0077 and “5 of 15”). The legs are 4 cm longer, so the crotch sits at 46.7 % of her height (45–48 % in the photos, 43.7 % before), and the bottom of the belly is 7.6 cm higher at the front (52.5 %; 52–53 % in the photos, 47.9 % before), so about 9 cm of the briefs shows under it. Only the lower half of the belly changed: the cups, teats, arms and head are exactly as they were, she is still 1.60 m tall, and the back keeps the shape you approved. The black belly patch now sits under the teats, as in the photos. The thighs meet the belly less in Walk, Run and Sneak; sitting down, the side of the belly brushes the thighs 0.5 mm more than before. (Round 18 is no longer in this viewer; its model stays in the handoff.)
  • Earlier · Gloves: now your own glove model at its real size: the cream terry mitten with its thumb, and the two black painted toe caps with their brush strokes and beaded rim. Each toe moves on its own, pivoting at the finger web: they curl together as before, and now part in a V like a Vulcan salute (see the arm fling in Exasperated). The thumb still bends on its own. The top of the palm bends with the toes, so no gap opens at the knuckles. The ambient occlusion baked into the glove's colour map is divided back out, so the viewer does all the shading.
  • Earlier · Sitting: Crouch and sit is replaced by Sit cross-legged. She steps one foot across the other, sinks down with her knees falling open, reaches back and lands heavily cross-legged, leans back with the belly resting on her legs, slumps with her forearms on her knees and sighs, then pushes up and steps out of it. In the old version the thighs went 5–6 cm up through the belly and hem, the seat sank into the floor and the arms pressed into the cups. New helper bones let the front of the hem ride up over a lifted thigh, as the fabric would. Now the belly stays within about 3 mm of the legs and the arms within 2 mm of the cups, and she sits on the floor, not in it. The other animations are unchanged.
  • Earlier · Slippers: now your own model of the real slippers: open-back cow mules with the white muzzle, smile and magenta nostrils, horns, ears with pearls, cream chenille head and black fringe. Because the heel is open, the tights now end in a black-socked foot standing on the footbed.
  • Earlier · Underwear joins in the middle: the back panel now runs down the centre into the crotch in one piece, so no strip of tights shows between the cheeks.
  • Gloves rebuilt: each glove is rebuilt as one smooth solid from your Tripo glove, with the same shape, thumb, hooves and texture but without the spiky edges, and with a clean round cuff that fits inside the sleeve instead of the ragged wrist.
  • Earlier · Underwear: the briefs are now grown from the tights' own surface, so they wrap them closely and move with them instead of floating. The leg openings are one smooth curve round each thigh, with no sharp corners front or back, and the sides sit lower, so the side panel shows below the belly from the front and while walking. The rolled hems tuck down onto the tights. The back keeps the shape you approved.
  • Earlier · Sides of the belly: the crease and bright spots down each side when the arms are raised are gone. The torso and inner sleeves were shaded as if the arm still hung against them.
  • No baked shadows: the model no longer carries any shading of its own; the dark band under the arms and the grey under the cups were baked in. All light and shadow now come from the viewer. Try the four rigs under Light.
  • Black shag: the patches are now grown as real strands instead of flat lock cards, which read as feathers. Each lock is a clump of about ten thin, tapered strands that leave the suit at an angle, droop and gather at the tip, over a short, tangled undercoat that gives the patch its volume. A few loose strays break up the outline. The strands are solid tubes, so they look the same from every side.
  • Round 11 · Underwear: the front is now cut like full briefs, from your reference photos and videos: each leg line runs from high on the hip down to a narrow gusset, so the front panel narrows into the crotch and the tights show in a V on each thigh. The back is unchanged.
  • Belly: the sides are rounded off, and the hem rises gently round the sides instead of cutting the belly off on a diagonal.
  • Teats: the holes at the tips of the two forward teats are closed.
  • Round 10: separated legs, underwear that follows the hips and crotch, the wrist flare, and hoof and thumb motion.
  • Tripo round: Blue Shirt’s udders, belly and hips; Shy Cow’s hooves, sleeve folds and knitted slippers with black socks.
  • Earlier: the tube on the mask’s top edge, lower and slimmer shoulders without a seam line, the crotch fix, the alt set with level eyes, and the mood animations.
