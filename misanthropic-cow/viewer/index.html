  • Round 37 (static preview): Neck: round 33’s neck shape with round 36’s neckline: the hood’s neck widens gently into the shoulders again (about 17 cm across just above the neckline), so the back of the neck slopes like a real nape instead of standing as a straight column, and it still goes down inside the costume, with no raised neck tube. The rolled neckline is a little wider, so the neck fits inside it, and now follows the top of the costume all the way round (in round 36 it stood above the sloping shoulders at the back). Pick Round 33, Round 36 and Round 37 with the Back or Side camera to compare.
  • Round 36 · Neck: a person’s head in a snug fabric hood, going down into the big padded costume, instead of one soft mound from the shoulders to the head. The hood’s neck is a human neck again, about 13 cm across, with the head’s jaw and the back of the skull defined above it and no flare spreading over the shoulders; it goes down inside the costume through a rolled neckline, a soft ring of the suit’s fur round the neck that sits a little lower at the front, under the mask’s chin. The suit no longer rises into a neck tube round it. The hood round the mask is round 35’s. Pick Round 35 and Round 36 and the Head or Back camera to compare.
  • Round 35 · Hood cleaned up: its edge round the mask is rounded over like the edge of a fabric opening instead of a square cut, so from the side and three-quarter views it wraps round the mask rather than looking sliced off; the head runs into the neck in one smooth sweep, with no ring crease under the jaw; and the hood’s fleece is smoother (its surface had the padded suit’s lumpy texture). Its size and shape are round 34’s. Pick Round 34 and Round 35 and the Head camera to compare.
  • Round 34 · Hood: fuller round the mask, as in the original 2012 shoot: from the front about 1–2 cm of fur now shows beside the mask on each side at eye level, rolling back round its edge instead of ending flat, and the neck under the snout is about 19 cm across (15–16 cm before). Top of the chest: the hard ledge and corner under the chin are gone: the neckline was cut flat across the front of the chest, which stands about 10 cm forward there; the top of the chest now curves smoothly from the neck down to the udders. Pick Round 33 and Round 34 to compare.

Hem: the bottom of the suit is 2.5 cm higher at the front and sides (0.81 m at the front, about 0.845 m at the sides), so about 10 cm of the briefs shows under the belly, as in the photos. Pick Round 32 and Round 33 to compare.

Udders and belly: a little smaller in front: each udder stands out about 1 cm less and is 4 mm narrower and shorter, and the front of the belly is about 1 cm less forward.
