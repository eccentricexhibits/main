UPLOADING TO TRIPO (multiview)
============================================================
  Front  <- Front.png
  Back   <- Back.png
  Left   <- Left_facing_left.png     (the cow faces the LEFT edge)
  Right  <- Right_facing_right.png   (the cow faces the RIGHT edge)

- All four are 1536x1536, pure white, same figure height (horn tips to slipper soles)
  and the same ground line. lineup_check.jpg shows them side by side.
- If the model comes out twisted, or with a face on two sides, swap Left and Right and
  run it again.
- Download the model as a zip (OBJ or GLB, with PBR textures) and send it back.

Known, and handled after Tripo (don't spend generations on them):
- Horns, udder nubs and the cord tail get modelled cleanly in Blender.
- The slippers get replaced by the finished slipper model from the slipper project.
- The far slipper sits a little high in both side views.
- The hands are ~11% smaller in the left-facing view than in the front.
- The back of the shorts gets painted at the texturing stage.
